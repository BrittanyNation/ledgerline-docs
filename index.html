How this documentation is written

Every guide follows the same shape: who it is for, what you need before you start, numbered steps, a worked example with real numbers, and the errors people hit most often. Plain words first, technical terms explained the first time they appear.

Guides are tested against the playground on the next page. If a step in a guide cannot be done in the playground, the guide is wrong and gets fixed first. That rule keeps the writing honest.
